# Ranyi head study

These orthographic images were rendered locally from the published Chibi Maid model by the dsh_blender_ranyimaid contributors, © 2026, under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). [Original Blender file](https://github.com/RYranyi/dsh_blender_ranyimaid/blob/59816e725a3258d3d63c168c12909ed5311792d0/chibi_maid_USER.blend) · [Author's license](https://github.com/RYranyi/dsh_blender_ranyimaid/blob/59816e725a3258d3d63c168c12909ed5311792d0/LICENSE)

A new orthographic camera and Workbench material colors with the Paint studio light were used for the study. The head and hair collections are isolated in the oblique and profile views. The base mesh view isolates the object named Head at the same scale and angle as the profile. Workbench shows the iris material color rather than its shaded vertex-color detail.
