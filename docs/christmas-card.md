# Christmas card

`christmas-card/` is an independent Vite entry. Edit the recipient, message lines and sender for both languages in `src/christmas-card/config.js`. The model can be rebuilt using the instructions in `models/christmas-envelope/README.md`. No new dependencies or external resources are used.

The card has a service worker scoped to its own path. Its build-time cache contains its HTML, manifest, icons and the Vite dependency closure including the GLB; upgrade installation waits until the visitor chooses Update now, even when old tabs close. A browser may terminate a long-pending installation; check for updates again if that happens. The gallery worker excludes card navigations and card clients from its update coordination, while its existing gallery scope and caches remain separate. A previously installed older gallery worker may still route the first card navigation to the gallery until that gallery worker is updated through the gallery's own update flow. Offline support requires one successful online card visit and completed worker installation.

The deployment selector discovers the GLB through the literal model import. Its cache key is calculated from committed Git-index entries; untracked changes cannot be verified as deployment inputs until added to the index. Publication remains a separate authorized action.

Snow and glowing gold stars accompany the loading status. Browsers with parallel shader compilation get up to 1.5 seconds of optional compilation and hidden draws. Other browsers start as soon as the model is ready. If startup is still pending after 12 seconds in the visible tab, the page displays the HTML greeting. Once ready and visible, the envelope approaches from beyond the left edge, grows in perspective, loops once through a stardust trail, then turns from the address side to the seal and settles. The flight normally takes about three seconds. Playback advances by at most 1/30 second per frame, and the envelope settles automatically after six visible seconds on slow devices. The address uses the configured recipient for the selected language. The Open button becomes available after the entrance. Replay restores the settled envelope and its idle camera. Reduced motion keeps the backdrop and loading stars still and skips the flight and animated opening.

The canvas fills the viewport. On resize, the camera computes three shots from fixed bounds for the envelope, flap, letter, and burst. It stays still during idle, then pushes close with a slight tilt as the flap opens. The mobile lens widens during the push-in to keep the full mouth in view. The camera eases back out for the burst and fully risen letter. The star and snow burst begins during the final part of the flap swing, and the letter starts rising during the burst. The HTML greeting appears with the envelope receding behind it. Metallic trim uses a locally generated Three.js room environment.
