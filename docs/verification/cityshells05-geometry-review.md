# City shells 05 C: bounded geometry review

Original assets are procedural and illustrative, not certified infrastructure, traffic layouts, operational equipment or real buildings. No third-party logos or assets were used.

## Fixed budgets and preserved failures

The initial strict structural log and affected source/export/preview snapshots are retained. Fruit rack geometry initially exceeded its 800,000-byte budget; fruit tessellation was reduced from 16x8 to 12x6. The condominium initially exceeded its 1,500,000-byte complex-shell budget; subpixel 2 mm bevels on its architectural boxes were removed. Neither budget nor dimension tolerance was increased. Complex shells were assigned a 40 m maximum dimension before generation; small props retain 10 m.

## Geometry corrections before final gates

- Wok controls now attach to an apron that meets the worktop.
- Transport ticket vending uses three fare buttons, a card reader, coin return and a horizontal retained 45 mm paper feed. It is not a queue-ticket dispenser.
- Petrol hose outer control points were adjusted to the original target envelope.
- Terrace-house upper floor ends at the wall interior, removing a coplanar seam; four rear windows were added. The entry leaf rotates about its fixed hinge.
- Shophouse floors end at inner wall faces and covered side galleries flank the true central airwell. Independent raycasts verify the central opening and adjacent covered strips.
- Car-park ramps were narrowed to avoid the outer structural columns. This does not certify vehicle circulation, grades or safety.
- Modern shoplot walls meet the roof slab without coplanar surfaces, and upper windows now attach to solid front backing panels.

## Semantic distinctions

The paired retail EAS antennae have an open centre, not a scanner portal. The floor fan has a cage and a continuously rotating axial rotor, not a ceiling mounting. The crossing has actual sloped kerb-cut approaches and tactile pads. The condominium includes a podium and 64 private balcony compartments. Shophouse arcade/airwell/pitched roofs differ from the modern flat-roof shoplot. A public market is a bounded hall shell with six counter bays, not a whole-world demonstration.

Final SHA-bound source, structural, component, animation, rendered-view and browser evidence lives with each asset. Physical Android/iOS and Safari/WebKit remain untested. No new Gallery component-control UI or production deployment is claimed.
