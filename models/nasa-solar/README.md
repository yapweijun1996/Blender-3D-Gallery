# NASA Solar System models

Eleven NASA models are provided as separately attributed gallery candidates: Sun, Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars, Jupiter, Uranus, Neptune, Pluto, Vesta and Eros. They do not replace the existing Blender Solar System models or alter the animated demo. See `provenance.json` for source pages, original URLs, SHA-256 hashes, credits, and original bounds. NASA's [media usage guidelines](https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/) apply. No NASA endorsement is implied.

The original GLBs are retained unchanged. Gallery models are centered and uniformly scaled to two units maximum extent. Earth, Mars, Vesta and Eros were additionally imported and re-exported with Blender's glTF exporter and tangents enabled to address the original files' missing-tangent validator warnings. Other gallery files have a root transform added without changing geometry or textures. Previews were rendered from the original GLBs; gallery exports require independent visual checks. Moon and comet models are not included because a corresponding downloadable whole-body NASA glTF was not confirmed. Vesta and Eros are real named bodies rather than replacements for the existing fictional asteroids.
