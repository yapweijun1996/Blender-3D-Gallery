# Roadside Catchpit Cutaway

Original Singapore-inspired roadside **illustration**, not a mapped site, design drawing, hydraulic simulation, specification or construction detail. The asphalt slice exposes a curb opening and separate grate connected to a deeper tapered catchpit and sump. The opening in the side wall and the connected outlet bore are hollow geometry. Flat water and small direction marks are illustrative; no pressure, flow capacity, dimensions for use, accessibility or engineering compliance is claimed.

- [PUB standard drawings index](https://www.pub.gov.sg/Professionals/Resources/Code-of-Practices/Standard-Drawings) lists roadside drainage components and drop-inlet chambers for regional feature context. [PUB's road-grating public explanation](https://www.pub.gov.sg/Resources/News-Room/Forum-Replies) documents road-surface drainage and transverse grate bars. [LTA road-element overview](https://www.lta.gov.sg/content/ltagov/en/industry_innovations/industry_matters/development_construction_specifications_resources/Transport_Infrastructure_Design_Criteria_and_Specifications.html) establishes the regional vocabulary. No official geometry, dimensions, text, insignia or drawings were reproduced.

## Source and verification

In this directory, use Blender 5.3.0: `blender -b -t 5 --python-exit-code 1 --python generate.py`. Use `-- --review /path/to/stills` for optional close views. The exact GLB was regenerated byte-for-byte. The editable source includes the isolated dry studio setup, never exported. Scene units are illustrative metres; Blender +Z up and −Y outward; glTF +Y up and +Z outward.

- `blender -b roadside-catchpit-cutaway.blend --python-exit-code 1 --python verify_blender.py` checks the editable source. `blender -b --python-exit-code 1 --python verify_blender.py -- --reimport` runs the same actual ray and welded-surface tests after reimporting the export.
- `node verify.mjs` applies the Khronos validator and Three.js loader/raycaster to the exported asset; no WebGL mock or bounding-box substitute for surfaces in the ray checks.
- Geometry checks cover nine bars, through-grate gaps, open kerb throat, the continuous catchpit chamber, and a bore through both outlet and sealed-around-aperture side wall. The gallery asset is static.
