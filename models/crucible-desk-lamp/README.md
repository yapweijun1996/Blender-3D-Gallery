# Low-Poly Desk Lamp

The original model was built in Crucible MCP 0.21.0. The [Crucible recipe](../../output/crucible-desk-lamp/desk-lamp.recipe.json) is the procedural source. The [Blender scene](desk-lamp.blend) preserves the editable meshes and materials imported from the [published GLB](desk-lamp.glb). The thumbnail was rendered from that import in Blender.

Blender 5.1.2 imported 15 mesh objects, 9 materials and 1,464 triangles. The lamp stands on Z = 0 and measures 254.9 × 180.0 × 331.2 mm in Blender X/Y/Z. The switch and hinges are modeled as static parts.

The Gallery uses the SHA-256-identical GLB from the [original export and browser validation](../../output/crucible-desk-lamp/README.md). Its SHA-256 is `a6ac194f611fdcce2c51db21ee8078de6c0af6d9035a9df6fb4eb170142c72ef`.
